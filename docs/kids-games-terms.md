## Games and purchases

Three games are free. The Full Version is a one-time purchase that opens all games in Piks Toddler Games. It does not renew. It applies only to this app.

Apple shows the price in your currency and processes the payment. To restore a purchase, use the Apple Account you used to buy it. In the app, open Parents and select Restore purchases. For payment and refund questions, contact [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/billing).
