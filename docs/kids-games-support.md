# Piks Toddler Games Support

## Play a game

Scroll the play tree to see more branches. Tap a picture to open a game. Tap the arrow in the upper left to return to the tree.

## Open settings

Tap Parents in the upper right of the play tree. Read the three number words. Enter their digits in the same order to open settings.

## Get help

If a game stops responding, close the app and open it again. Avoid deleting the app if you want to keep your saved progress.

For help, email [anq.no.one@gmail.com](mailto:anq.no.one@gmail.com). Include the app version, your device model, and a short description of the issue. Please do not include a child’s personal information.
