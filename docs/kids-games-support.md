# Piks Toddler Games Support

## Play a game

Scroll the Game Tree. Tap a picture to play. Tap the back arrow in the upper left to return to the tree.

Picnic, Picture shapes, and Bath time are free. Games work offline. You need an internet connection to buy or restore the Full Version.

## Buy or restore the Full Version

To buy, tap a locked game. The offer shows the price and what the purchase includes. Select Unlock and answer the addition problem. Then confirm the purchase with Apple.

To restore, tap Parents in the Game Tree and answer the addition problem. Select Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that bought the Full Version.

## Contact support

Email [anq.no.one@gmail.com](mailto:anq.no.one@gmail.com). Include your app version, device model, and a short description of the problem. Please leave out your child’s personal information.
