# Piks Toddler Games Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026.

Piks Toddler Games is a game for young children. The app does not collect or transmit personal data. It does not use ads, tracking, or third-party analytics. The app does not require an account.

The app saves game progress and settings on your device. You can erase this data by deleting the app. The games work without an internet connection.

If you email support, we use your email address and message to reply and resolve the issue. Please do not include a child’s name or other personal information.

Apple manages app downloads and handles that information under its own privacy policy.

For privacy questions, email [anq.no.one@gmail.com](mailto:anq.no.one@gmail.com).
