# Toddler Games: Piks Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026.

## The app

Toddler Games: Piks does not collect personal data or require an account. The app has no ads, tracking, or third-party analytics.

The app saves game progress and preferences on your device. You can remove this local data by deleting the app. Games can be played offline.

## Purchases and ratings

Apple processes app downloads, payments, restores, and ratings. The app asks Apple to verify a purchase before it opens the games. The app does not receive your payment card details. See [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support

If you email us, we use your address and message to reply and resolve the issue. Please leave out your child’s name and other personal information. To ask a privacy question or request deletion of your support correspondence, email [anq.no.one@gmail.com](mailto:anq.no.one@gmail.com).
